Helpful corrections from your class notes

  • mai can mean not, or mark a question. The spoken tones differ, although we keep simple letters here.
  • gai means chicken; khai means egg. Easy to confuse when ordering!
  • khao can mean rice or he/she depending on pronunciation and tone.
  • mai pen rai means it's okay / no problem and can also be a polite response to thanks.
  • khaw long trong nee means I'd like to get off here. long (get off) and lawng (try) are different words.
  • These are classroom-style English-letter approximations, not a precise pronunciation system. Some spellings are normalized for clarity.
